# Content Rights Schedule 2026

**Owner:** Content Licensing and Legal
**Status:** Binding. Supersedes product documentation where the two disagree.
**Applies to:** all titles in the StreamRights demo catalogue

## Purpose

This schedule records the obligations we accepted from licensors. Product
behaviour must satisfy these terms. Where a product document describes
behaviour that conflicts with a term here, this schedule governs.

## Terms

### T1 - Licence windows are time bounded

Every licence grant carries a window start date and a window end date. A title
is licensed in a territory only between those two dates inclusive. Outside the
window the grant does not exist, regardless of territory or tier.

### T2 - Pre-window titles must be surfaced, not hidden

For titles whose window start date is in the future, the licensor requires a
promotional placement. The title appears in the browse list for the licensed
territory, marked as coming soon with its window start date shown. It is not
playable before the window opens.

### T3 - Post-window titles are removed entirely

Once a window end date has passed, the title is removed from the browse list and
its detail page is no longer reachable in that territory. No promotional
placement is permitted after expiry.

### T4 - Certain grants are ad-supported tier only

A subset of grants are licensed for ad-supported distribution only. These titles
are available to viewers on the Free tier and are not available on Standard or
Premium, because the licensor sells the paid-tier rights separately.

### T5 - Blocked playback must state the governing reason

Where playback is refused, the viewer is told which of the three grounds
applies: territory, tier, or licence window. A generic unavailable message does
not satisfy the licensor's reporting obligation.

### T6 - Territory is determined at playback, not at browse

Entitlement is evaluated against the viewer's territory at the moment the play
request is made. A catalogue list rendered earlier under a different territory
does not confer a right to play.
